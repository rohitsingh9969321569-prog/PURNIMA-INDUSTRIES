About The Works

Built on the shop floor, not a brochure

Started in 2024 on the principle that quality work earns repeat customers. Purnima Industries specialises in precision laser cutting and sheet metal fabrication out of a 2,500 sq. ft. facility in Ambernath MIDC, built to keep pace with how fast engineering requirements change today.

Our capabilities span sheet metal fabrication, structural fabrication, industrial contracting and spare parts manufacturing. A small, hands-on team means every job gets direct attention from cutting through to the finished part.

What We Do

One shop floor, every process your drawing needs

From a single flat-pattern DXF to a finished, coated assembly — cutting, bending and fabrication under one roof, with cable tray manufacturing as a dedicated line.

01

Laser Cutting

MS, SS & Aluminium up to 25mm, Brass up to 10mm, Copper up to 5mm — cut from your DXF/DWG.

02

CNC Bending & Punching

4-axis CNC press brake, 3100mm bend length, plus CNC punching for panels, brackets and enclosures.

03

Fabrication & Structural Work

Structural fabrication, industrial contracting, MIG welding and mechanical assembly, start to finish.

04

Cable Tray Manufacturing

Perforated and ladder-type cable trays, flanges, fittings and brackets — cut and formed in-house.

05

OEM & Custom Manufacturing

Custom manufacturing for OEMs, food-grade stainless steel work, and industrial machinery spares.

06

Powder Coating Finish

Coating arranged through our finishing partner as part of your order — one point of contact.
